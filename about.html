About this edition

This website presents a digital sample edition of titulus 1 de simonia (quaestiones 1–16) of the second distinctio of the Summa quaestionum ‘De quaestionibus decretalibus tractaturi’ of Magister Honorius. It is a working draft; the encoding of further tituli is planned.

Text, critical apparatus and apparatus fontium follow the print edition: Magistri Honorii Summa Quaestionum ‘De quaestionibus decretalibus tractaturi’ (D.2), ed. Niels Becker, Waltraud Kozur, Karin Miethaner-Vent, Würzburg 2021 (honorius-summa-quaestionum.com). Line and page numbers are those of the print edition. Corrections of the print edition by the editor are marked with an asterisk (*) in the apparatus; pointing at it shows the details.

The critical apparatus records the Leithandschrift P in full. The manuscripts La, L, Z, B and D are recorded only for passages containing allegations and for the structural elements (Queritur, Videtur, Econtra, Solutio etc.).

TEI encoding and digital presentation: Niels Becker.

Using the edition

  • Reading view and Parallel view (the text beside the Leithandschrift P, Paris, BnF, lat. 14591, with images from Gallica); “q.1”, “q.2” etc. in the parallel view show the opening question of each quaestio in P.
  • Two modes: Variants (select a word to see its entry in the critical apparatus) and Sources (allegations and passages with a note in the apparatus fontium); allegations of the Decretum Gratiani link to the online edition (BSB/MGH).
  • Critical apparatus and apparatus fontium after each quaestio, as in the print edition; indices of persons, allegations and manuscripts.
  • Cite suggests a citation of the current quaestio; TEI-XML downloads the encoded text.
